Data Provenance Initiative: an audit collection and measurement systems. Operator: the Data Provenance Initiative (a volunteer collective of AI researchers; the founding paper by Shayne Longpre and 16 other authors). The object it attaches provenance to: a fine-tuning dataset, traced through its collection to its sources.

The Data Provenance Initiative is a multi-disciplinary volunteer effort to improve transparency, documentation, and responsible use of training datasets for AI.
