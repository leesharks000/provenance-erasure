Getty Provenance Index: a database (linked open data). Operator: Getty Research Institute (J. Paul Getty Trust). The object it attaches provenance to: a work of art and the documents of its ownership and sale.

The Getty Provenance Index® (GPI) contains over 12 million digitized, open-access resources that trace the origins, movements, and lineages of art and cultural artifacts.
