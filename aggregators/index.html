Provenance meta-aggregator

The resources that carry, record and test provenance, from art history and manuscript studies to media credentials and model training data, each formalized as an entity. Every record answers one question: what provenance does this system carry, through which operation, on whose assertion, with what evidence of survival? 14 resources, compiled 2026-10-29.

What provenance: the object that receives it (a file, a dataset, an assertion, a token, a manuscript, a work of art), the relations carried (creation, credit, rights, ownership, derivation, support) and what carries them (embedded metadata, a signed manifest, a graph, a revision history, a transcribed document).

Through which operation: the transformations the resource actually tested, each with its date: an upload and a download, publication through a news pipeline, every edit of an article, a model's generation.

On whose assertion: every field is tagged with its basis: the operator's own statement, a third-party account, our observation, or our reading. Each quote opens with its source and the hash of the copy read.

With what evidence of survival: what a dated test shows surviving. A resource that expresses or records provenance without testing it reads not tested, never a failure.

Each record keeps apart what a resource can express, what it records, and what it tests. Tap a step for the fields that answer it.

AttriBench
Answer enginesbenchmarkpublished
AttriBench is a fame- and demographically-balanced quote attribution benchmark for measuring attribution bias in large language models, introduced by Eliza Berman, Bella Chang, Daniel B. Neill and Emily Black in a paper accepted at COLM 2026. Its unit is a quote of five to thirty words with its canonical author.

AttributionBench
Answer enginesbenchmarkpublished
AttributionBench is a benchmark of attribution evaluation, testing whether every claim in a generated response is fully supported by its cited evidence, built by Yifei Li, Xiang Yue, Zeyi Liao and Huan Sun of The Ohio State University and accepted to ACL 2024 Findings. Its unit is a claim–evidence pair with a binary attribution label.

C2PA Conformance Program and Explorer
Media and imagesregistryactive
The C2PA Conformance Program is the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity's risk-based governance program that designates generator and validator products as conforming and holds certification authorities to the C2PA Certificate Policy. Administered by the C2PA Conformance Task Force, it opened in June 2025 and is now at version 0.2; its unit is the product on the public Conforming Products List.

Data Provenance Initiative
Models and training dataaudit collection and measurement systemsactive
The Data Provenance Initiative is a volunteer collective of AI researchers auditing the datasets, web policies, model markets and real-world uses that shape AI systems, dating from its founding audit of October 2023 by Shayne Longpre and 16 other authors. Its unit is a dataset within a collection, traced through its collection to its sources, creators and licences.

Getty Provenance Index
Objects and artdatabase (linked open data)active
The Getty Provenance Index is the Getty Research Institute's database of art provenance: over 12 million digitized, open-access resources, drawn from archival records of the 16th to the 20th centuries, that document historic transfers of art ownership. Its unit is a resource drawn from a primary document, and the Index is moving to linked open data on the Arches platform.

IPTC photo metadata tests
Media and imagestest serieshistorical
The IPTC photo metadata tests are the IPTC Photo Metadata Working Group's checks of what image-sharing sites and systems for sharing files and messages do to the metadata embedded in an uploaded photograph, run in 2013, 2016 and Spring 2019. Their unit is the site or system, and the carrier they test is the Exif and IPTC metadata embedded in the file.

Mapping Manuscript Migrations
Manuscripts and booksaggregator (linked open data)project ended
Mapping Manuscript Migrations (MMM) is a linked open data aggregator of the history and provenance of medieval and Renaissance manuscripts, combining the Schoenberg Database of Manuscripts, Bibale and Medieval Manuscripts in Oxford Libraries into one knowledge graph. A Digging into Data project of four institutions funded between 2017 and 2020, it has ended, and its data are maintained.

Nanopublications
The scholarly recordpublication model and service ecosystemactive
A nanopublication is a small knowledge graph snippet with metadata, treated as an independent scientific publication: an assertion with its provenance and its publication info, implemented in RDF and published to a decentralized server network. The model was conceived from 2009 and is kept by the nanopublication community at nanopub.net.

OLMoTrace
Models and training datatracing toolactive
OLMoTrace is Ai2's tool that traces the outputs of language models back to their full, multi-trillion-token training data in real time, showing verbatim matches between spans of output and training documents. Introduced on 9 April 2025 as a feature of the Ai2 Playground, its unit is a maximal matching span of model output.

ProvStore was a free public repository for W3C PROV provenance documents, storing, browsing and managing them through a web interface or a REST API. Led by Dong Huynh, it ran at the University of Southampton from 2013 and at King's College London from 2018, and was retired in December 2024.

VroniPlag Wiki is a volunteer wiki, started on 28 March 2011 at Wikia (now Fandom), that examines and documents plagiarism in German doctoral theses and other academic writings. Its unit is a thesis, documented as fragments that set each passage beside the source it reproduces without attribution.

WikiWho
Authorship and reusealgorithm and APIactive
WikiWho is an algorithm and API that attributes authorship of Wikipedia articles at the level of the token, giving for every word the revision and editor that introduced it and every removal and reintroduction since. First developed at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology and GESIS, it has been maintained since August 2021 by Wikimedia Community Tech and the Wiki Education Foundation.
