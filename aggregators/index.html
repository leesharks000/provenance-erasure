Provenance meta-aggregator

The resources that carry, record and test provenance, from art history and manuscript studies to media credentials and model training data, each formalized as an entity. Every record answers one question: what provenance does this system carry, through which operation, on whose assertion, with what evidence of survival? 14 resources, compiled 2026-10-29.

What provenance: the object that receives it (a file, a dataset, an assertion, a token, a manuscript, a work of art), the relations carried (creation, credit, rights, ownership, derivation, support) and what carries them (embedded metadata, a signed manifest, a graph, a revision history, a transcribed document).

Through which operation: the transformations the resource actually tested, each with its date: an upload and a download, publication through a news pipeline, every edit of an article, a model's generation.

On whose assertion: every field is tagged with its basis: the operator's own statement, a third-party account, our observation, or our reading. Each quote opens with its source and the hash of the copy read.

With what evidence of survival: what a dated test shows surviving. A resource that expresses or records provenance without testing it reads not tested, never a failure.

Each record keeps apart what a resource can express, what it records, and what it tests. Tap a step for the fields that answer it.
