IPTC Metawatch

Media and imagesmonitoractive
IPTC Metawatch: a monitor. Operator: IPTC. The object it attaches provenance to: the lead photograph of a news article, as served to the reader.
Visit IPTC Metawatch ↗Run an erasure audit
a free, open monitor that checks every month whether more than 500 news publishers in 122 countries keep the credits, captions and rights information embedded in their photographs

In its own words: https://iptc.org/news/announcing-iptc-metawatch/, read 2026-10-29
