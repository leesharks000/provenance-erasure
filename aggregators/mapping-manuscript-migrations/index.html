Mapping Manuscript Migrations

Manuscripts and booksaggregator (linked open data)project ended
Mapping Manuscript Migrations: an aggregator (linked open data). Operator: University of Oxford, University of Pennsylvania (Schoenberg Institute for Manuscript Studies), IRHT-CNRS, Aalto University. The object it attaches provenance to: a medieval or Renaissance manuscript and the events of its history.
Visit Mapping Manuscript Migrations ↗The /non rowRun an erasure audit
Its main goal is to combine data from several disparate sources about medieval and Renaissance manuscripts, and to use the aggregated data to explore a range of research questions about their history and provenance.

In its own words: https://seco.cs.aalto.fi/publications/2020/burrows-et-al-mmm-white-paper.pdf, read 2026-10-29
