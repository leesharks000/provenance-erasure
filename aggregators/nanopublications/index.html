Nanopublications: a publication model and service ecosystem. Operator: the nanopublication community (nanopub.net). The object it attaches provenance to: an assertion: a small knowledge-graph snippet published on its own.
Visit Nanopublications ↗Run an erasure audit
A nanopublication is a small knowledge graph snippet with metadata that is treated as an independent (scientific) publication.
