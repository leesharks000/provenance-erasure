OLMoTrace

Models and training datatracing toolactive
OLMoTrace: a tracing tool. Operator: Allen Institute for AI (Ai2), with the University of Washington, UC Berkeley and Stanford. The object it attaches provenance to: a span of a language model's output and the training documents that contain it verbatim.
Visit OLMoTrace ↗The /non rowRun an erasure audit
We present OLMoTrace, the first system that traces the outputs of language models back to their full, multi-trillion-token training data in real time.

In its own words: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07096, read 2026-10-29
