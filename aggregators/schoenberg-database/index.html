Schoenberg Database of Manuscripts

Manuscripts and booksdatabaseactive
Schoenberg Database of Manuscripts: a database. Operator: Schoenberg Institute for Manuscript Studies, University of Pennsylvania. The object it attaches provenance to: an observation of a pre-modern manuscript in a catalogue, inventory or other source.
Visit Schoenberg Database of Manuscripts ↗The /non rowRun an erasure audit
The SDBM continuously aggregates and updates historic observations of pre-modern manuscripts drawn from over 14,000 auction and sales catalogs, inventories, catalogs from institutional and private collections, and other sources that document the sales and locations of these books from around the world.
