WikiWho

Authorship and reusealgorithm and APIactive
WikiWho: an algorithm and API. Operator: first developed by Karlsruhe Institute of Technology and GESIS; since August 2021 maintained at Wikimedia, by Community Tech and the Wiki Education Foundation (the MediaWiki page's text) or Moderator Tools and the Wiki Education Foundation (its infobox). The object it attaches provenance to: a single token (word) of a Wikipedia article.
Visit WikiWho ↗The /non rowRun an erasure audit
The WikiWho API gives access to the provenance/changes of words (tokens) in Wikipedia articles.

In its own words: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gesiscss/wikiwho_api_public/master/README.md, read 2026-10-29

What it carries

What receives provenancea single token (word) of a Wikipedia article
Relations carriedorigin (the revision and editor that introduced the token); deletion; reintroduction op
Carrierthe article's full revision history, re-parsed
Expresses · records · testsexpressesrecordstests
Operations testedevery edit in an article's revision history
What survivedper token: when it entered, who wrote it, every removal and reinsertion; method evaluated at 95% on revision-rich articles op
