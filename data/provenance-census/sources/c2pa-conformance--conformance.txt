 Conformance

 Welcome!

You have reached the landing page of the C2PA Conformance Program. After opening the program in June 2025, we have upgraded our conformance program to v0.2.

 The C2PA Conformance Program provides assurance that products adhere to the Content Credentials specification, and fulfill a set of security requirements to ensure they are producing and validating C2PA data correctly. The Program is part of a C2PA Governance Framework governed by the C2PA Steering Committee and is administered by the C2PA Conformance Task Force.

 This Conformance Program is a risk-based, transparent and unbiased governance process intended to hold generator products, validator products and certification authorities accountable to the Content Credentials specification, the  Certificate Policy  and the  Security Requirements . Conforming products are placed on a publicly-accessible list. This conveys confidence in the implementation and its security to the public and guarantees interoperability across products in the Content Credentials ecosystem.
