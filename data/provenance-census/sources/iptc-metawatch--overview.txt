 Metawatch

Metawatch tracks how often major news publishers preserve embedded photo metadata
 (Exif, IPTC Photo Metadata (in both original IIM format and newer XMP format), and C2PA) on the images they publish.
 We crawl a curated list of publishers monthly and score the photographs they serve.

Got a publisher we should add, a metric we're missing, or a number that looks wrong?
 Tell us via this short form 
— we read every response.
