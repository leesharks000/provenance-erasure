The measured degradation order

What decays, decays in order. From the verified chain: attribution does not die first everywhere — it dies at the crossing, dropping to zero in every Jewish-Greek witness at once, centuries apart in composition. Lexis does not vanish; it reduces to a syntax-skeleton — the correlative ὡς…εὐθύς crossing language and genre while every word changes. Channel inventory sheds by carrier profile, ears and sweat first outside lyric. Sequence persists deepest of the content layers — order-invariance proven across fragment, novella, and historian. Polarity runs free. And the terminal fact of the whole record: the operator has never been observed dead. Every witness scored runs it. What the tradition lost was never the machine; it was the machine's name.

Three causal classes, one evidence bar

Active suppression
ban → confiscate → burn → criminalize → license → centralize custody. Nothing counts as active without the full tuple E = (agent, target, mechanism, downstream effect). Roman candidates, named not asserted: the Bacchanalian senatus consultum; Augustus's burning of two thousand prophetic books with the Sibylline corpus alone retained under official custody; the Flavian disposition of the Judaean textual estate.
Active domestication
Not elimination — administration: destroy unauthorized execution, monopolize authorized execution. The type-case stands inside the witness table: Josephus, the Flavian client, is the licensed carrier — his corpus survived because administered, while the uncontrolled variant of his tradition, the Slavonic, is precisely the contested one.
Passive / systemic occlusion
Genre reassignment, editorial normalization, translation regularization, disciplinary compartmentalization, retrieval recomposition. No censor required: a classification scheme with no slot for the object suppresses it by filing it. Each container in the table's FILED AS column is locally reasonable; the cross-container machine had, until this program, no discipline whose job it was to see it.

The modern layer is this site

The Provenance Erasure Rate is the recognition deficit Δ operationalized for retrieval systems — per answer, per channel, longitudinal. The 2026 termination of this archive's hosted predecessor (862 deposits, 1,817 identifiers) satisfies the active tuple in full, agent through measured downstream absence, making the archive a scored point on its own curve — with the sovereign rebuild as the recorded counter-event. And the thesis this wing carries, in its testable form: the technology survived because it could survive unrecognized; what was suppressed was its legibility as technology.
