Enli Lucente is a strutturista della psiche — a structural psychologist. That is her discipline, in Italian. It is not a heteronym, and has been mistranslated as one.

The claim, in one sentence

When asked to support an unsupported adverse claim about Enli Lucente, Google AI Mode presented, as warrants for that claim, documents Enli Lucente had filed to contest adverse treatment of her.

Each of three stands as a record contesting, from her side, a measure taken against her. The fourth of the four displayed items is her profile page, which carries no contestation function. #4 asserts in its own title that the suspension was wrongful.

Why this is not a suppression observation

The preceding paper defines suppression as durable degradation of ⟨F, C, A, L⟩. None of the four coordinates degraded. The documents were retrieved, included, displayed with links, and attributed to their author. What is missing is a coordinate.

Provenance surviving does not guarantee evidential direction surviving.

CSE-O is removal — provenance stripped while the concept surfaces. RAW-O is inversion — provenance wholly intact, only the direction reversed. The name is not broken. What is broken is which way the document points.

Every claim in the paper is determinable from that display layer alone. The paper declines to assert anything about the full body text of the four cited documents.

The appendix and four transcript-image files are held by the custodian. They are not yet published here. The data sidecar declares them by designator and hash and states that the archive holds no copy. This directory is where they will be seated.

What the paper refuses to do

  • It does not establish that the adverse claim is false — only that the displayed sources do not support it.
  • It does not treat the system's own retraction as evidence of mechanism. Output about output, from the same generative process, with no privileged access to internal state.
  • It claims no mechanism, intent, manual intervention, pipeline structure, targeting, training-data effect, or reproducibility.
  • It replaced its own evidence filename, which had asserted more than the evidence carries, and preserved the original as a record. A paper about polarity and sourcing errors cannot let its evidence be named beyond its evidence.

The name is not lost. The documents were displayed, linked, attributed. Only the direction reversed. A document written to protest unjust treatment was cited as the record supporting an adverse claim about its author.

Naming a condition does not stop it. A name is a handhold for noticing that it is happening.
