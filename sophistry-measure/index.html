The definition it is built from. ἔστι γὰρ ἡ σοφιστικὴ φαινομένη σοφία οὖσα δ᾿ οὔ, καὶ ὁ σοφιστὴς χρηματιστὴς ἀπὸ φαινομένης σοφίας ἀλλ᾿ οὐκ οὔσης (SE 1). Sophistry is wisdom that appears and is not, and the sophist makes money from the appearance. The measure records, at an address, the gap between what a composition presents itself as carrying and what it carries. Each finding is one transformation, reconstructed from the texts, with its witness. It has no scalar.

Why it stands beside PER. PER measures declared losses against a versioned target: author, institution, identifier, source, and in its dimensional form (#1469) the atoms of the work (author, title, date, claim boundary), of its context (framework, tradition, institution) and of its depth (lineage, identifiers, licence). A composition can keep every token PER counts and still change the authority, scope or relation under which it presents them. The measure's case is an observation scored at PER 0.042, eleven of twelve atoms present, every quotation genuine, which substitutes one locus of a concept developed across eight deposits for the concept. Quotation verification cannot settle representational adequacy. The measure types that transformation (X24, locus substituted for entity) and examines the warrant of the measurement target itself.

The governing condition. The composition's claim to adequacy. A short answer drawn from one source is ordinary selection; it becomes a finding where the composition claims, in words or by its presentation, to stand for more than its selection warrants. Each finding records what was asked, what scope the answer announces, and which omitted relation materially changes the answer at that scope, before any operation is typed.
